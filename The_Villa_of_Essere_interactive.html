THE GOLDEN RULE — ask these three questions, in this order
  1. 1Is there a reflexive pronoun? (mi, ti, si, ci, vi) mi sono alzato · ci siamo divertitiESSERE
  2. 2Does the verb take a direct object? (…what? …who?) ho mangiato la pizza · ho aiutato MarcoAVERE
  3. 3Does the sentence say where the subject IS, where it ENDS UP, or what it BECOMES? sono rimasto a casa · sono andato a Roma · sono diventato medicoESSERE

If the answer to 3 is no — the sentence only says what the subject DOES — then it is AVERE.

MOTION VERBS — the same action, two different auxiliaries

Both groups describe movement. What changes is whether the verb names a destination.

DIRECTED MOTION → ESSEREMANNER OF MOTION → AVERE
What the verb tells youWHERE you end up — there is a destinationHOW you move — there is no destination

The same verb, both roomssono corso a casa · è volato a Parigiho corso per un'ora · ha volato per ore

In short: a destination puts the verb inside the villa. No destination leaves it outside — and a direct object always sends it outside.
